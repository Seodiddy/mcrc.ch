// MCRC Concierge · recommendation engine
import { protocols, studies, datasheets } from "@mcrc/knowledge";

const guest = listen({ profile: "athlete", goal: "recovery" });
const options = ["CRYO", "HUMAN REGENERATOR", "RED LIGHT", "HBOT", "SAUNA"];

for (const t of options) {
  if (contraindicated(t, guest)) continue;   // contraindications checked
  const fit = score(t, guest.goal, guest.profile);
  ranked.push({ technology: t, fit, sources: cite(studies, "peer-reviewed studies") });
}

const CRYO = { temperature: "-110 °C", session: "2–3 min", before: "SAUNA" };
const HBOT = { pressure: "1.5–2.0 ATA", seats: 2, mood: "deep pressure, calm breath" };
const RED_LIGHT = { spectrum: "red & near-infrared", position: "zero gravity" };
const HUMAN_REGENERATOR = { field: "cold plasma", body: "full", origin: "Made in Germany" };
const SAUNA = { ritual: "rounds & rituals", rhythm: "heat, then cold", rounds: 3 };

answer({
  recommendation: ranked[0].technology,   // "HBOT"
  order: ["HBOT", "RED LIGHT", "CRYO"],
  step: "protocol · step 2",
  when: "after training, before sleep",
  why: explain(ranked[0], guest),
  sources: ranked[0].sources,
  language: detect(guest)                  // en · de · es · fr
});

offer({ slot: calendar.next(), package: match(guest), signup: false });

// next guest
const couple = listen({ profile: "couple", goal: "deep sleep" });
const evening = plan(couple, ["SAUNA", "RED LIGHT", "HUMAN REGENERATOR"]);
if (couple.first_visit) brief(team, evening, { tone: "warm" });

check(protocols.safety, ["CRYO", "HBOT"], couple);
answer({ recommendation: "SAUNA", then: "RED LIGHT", sources: cite(studies) });

// manufacturer view
const device = datasheets.get("HUMAN REGENERATOR", "manufacturer datasheet");
certify(device, "TÜV NORD");
train(operators, device, { languages: ["en", "de", "es", "fr"] });
const h2 = datasheets.get("S 3000 Pro", { status: "Swiss registered" });
const faq = answers.from(device, protocols, { claims: "market-safe" });

stream(answer).to(guest.screen);  // never sleeps · never forgets

01 / Introduction

What it is

The art of the perfect recommendation. Automated.

Imagine a concierge who knows every treatment, every technology, every protocol and the science behind them. Who listens with genuine attention, explains complicated things beautifully and always knows what to suggest next.

Available around the clock, in your guest's language, with the warmth of a personal conversation and the expertise of your best specialist.

For spas, hotels and longevity centres, it becomes a personal guest advisor, turning curiosity into appointments and bookings. For manufacturers, it brings product expertise to every buyer, partner and venue, supporting sales, education and daily operations.

All in your own brand, with your own voice, directly on your website.

02 / The problem

Too much choice

Wonderful service begins with understanding.

Your guests see a menu full of possibilities: cold, light, oxygen, hydrogen, compression. Which one suits me? In which order? How often? And why? With every new option, the choice becomes harder. In a famous experiment, a table with 24 jams attracted more visitors, but only 3% bought. With 6 jams, 30% did.(1)

Your team faces another challenge. New technologies, new studies, countless applications and combinations. It takes months, sometimes years, to master them. And questions rarely arrive at the perfect moment: the team is busy, the working day is over, or the guest is in another time zone.

For your guestsPerformance Concierge listens, understands, explains and inspires, turning overwhelming choice into the pleasure of finding exactly what feels right.

For your teamA knowledgeable companion, always available to answer questions, share expertise and help every colleague grow more confident.

(1) Iyengar SS, Lepper MR, Journal of Personality and Social Psychology, 2000

09 / Good to know

Good to know

What is Performance Concierge?

A concierge that combines human expertise with artificial intelligence, for wellness, longevity and performance venues and the brands that make their equipment. It listens to each visitor, answers from verified knowledge, and leads to the right booking or product.

How is it different from a chatbot?

Most chatbots either follow a fixed script or improvise from what a general AI model has learned. Performance Concierge works from knowledge that experts have curated and verified: manufacturer and expert FAQs, SOPs, datasheets, studies and protocols. It answers with a service attitude, adapts its claims to each market, and guides every visitor to a clear next step.

Where does its knowledge come from?

From you and from experts: your FAQs, SOPs, datasheets and protocols, combined with manufacturer knowledge and published research. Your expertise speaks in every answer.

How can AI help guests choose between treatments?

By asking before it answers. Two short questions about profile and goal, and the Concierge explains which treatments suit, in which order, how often and why. Guests arrive informed and curious instead of lost in the menu.

Does it replace my staff?

No, it gives them time. It answers around the clock, prepares guests before their visit and briefs your team with the same knowledge, so your people can focus on a warm welcome and a real conversation.

How can manufacturers support operators after installation?

Every guest gets the same clear, expert answers about your device, in every venue and every language. The team at each venue is briefed with the same knowledge, and every answer stays true to your science. The sale becomes the beginning of the relationship.

Which languages does it speak?

English, German, Spanish and French out of the box. Any other language on request.

Do visitors have to sign up?

No. No sign-up and no email gate: 2 short questions, and the conversation begins.

Is it GDPR-compliant?

Yes. No personal data is stored without consent, everything is hosted in the EU, and conversation logs are available on request.

How do we get started?

With one form. You fill it in, add your knowledge, and we send you the link. Want to see it first? We'll set up a live demo with your own technologies.

How is it priced?

In 2 models. Centres pay a licence per location. Manufacturers pay one annual licence plus activation per operator. We'll take you through the details personally.

Concierge answers are informational and no substitute for medical or professional advice.
